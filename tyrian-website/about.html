We are a focused trading company with a singular mission — making global commodity trade simple, transparent, and reliable for every partner.

Who We Are

A Young Company with Seasoned Expertise

Tyrian Global was established with a clear ambition: to bring professionalism, transparency, and genuine partnership to the cement and steel trading industry. Despite being a dynamic and growing company, our leadership and core team carry decades of combined experience in commodity trading, international logistics, and supply chain management.

We believe that great trading relationships are built on trust — trust in product quality, trust in delivery timelines, and trust in the people behind the deal. That is why at Tyrian Global, we never compromise on any of these.

Operating from Dubai — the crossroads of global trade — we are strategically positioned to source from leading factories in Europe, Asia, and beyond, delivering to buyers across Africa, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas.

Our Mission

To simplify international procurement of cement and steel by providing partners with a single, reliable point of contact — from sourcing and quality verification to logistics and documentation.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in commodity trading within our categories — recognized for quality, speed, and the depth of our partner relationships on every continent.

Our Expertise

Three Pillars of Trade Mastery

Our team's expertise spans three critical disciplines that are essential to successful commodity trade. We don't just source products — we orchestrate the entire commercial journey.

💼

Trading & Procurement

We have built a global network of verified factories and suppliers in cement and steel. Our buyers negotiate from a position of deep market knowledge — securing the best price, grade, and availability for every order.

🚢

Shipping & Freight

From bulk vessel charters to full-container-load bookings, our logistics team handles all freight operations. We coordinate with agents, port authorities, and freight forwarders to ensure smooth, on-schedule deliveries.

📋

Trade Documentation

International trade demands precise documentation. Our experienced team manages letters of credit, Bills of Lading, certificates of analysis, SGS/Intertek inspection reports, and customs compliance — flawlessly.
