Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary by product. For bagged cement, minimum is typically 1 x 20ft container (approx. 25 MT). For bulk shipments, minimums start from 5,000 MT. Contact us with your specific requirement.

What Incoterms do you offer?

We offer FOB, CFR, CIF, and DAP terms depending on the product and destination. We recommend CIF for new partners to simplify the initial transaction.

What payment methods do you accept?

We work with Letters of Credit (LC at sight), T/T (bank transfer), and for established partners, open account terms may be available. We do not accept cash payments.

How quickly can you respond to a quote request?

We acknowledge all inquiries within 24 hours and typically provide a full competitive offer within 48 hours of receiving your complete specifications.

Do you provide quality inspection certificates?

Yes — all shipments are accompanied by independent inspection reports from SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas, as well as mill certificates and certificates of analysis.
