Cement Products

Bagged, bulk and specialty cement products for every construction application

Grey Cement (Bagged)

Best Seller

Our most traded product. Years of operational experience make bagged grey cement shipments seamless for our partners. Sourced from certified European and Asian factories, each lot comes with full quality documentation.

Available Grades
CEM I 52.5 N CEM I 52.5 R CEM I 42.5 R CEM I 42.5 R SR CEM II A-M 42.5 R CEM III A 42.5 N

Bulk Cement

Bulk cement delivered by vessel in pneumatic tankers or by road tanker to cement plants and ready-mix operations. Ideal for large-scale construction projects requiring continuous supply. Compliant with ASTM and EN standards.

Available Grades
ASTM Type I/II CEM I 52.5 N CEM I 42.5 R CEM II A-M 42.5 R

White Cement

High-purity white Portland cement for architectural, decorative, and specialty concrete applications. Manufactured to exacting whiteness standards using carefully selected raw materials with minimal iron oxide content.

Available Grades
CEM I 52.5 R CEM II B-LL 42.5 R Whiteness ≥85%

Clinker

Bulk cement clinker sourced from a diverse network of global factories. The challenge in clinker is matching quality expectations with competitive pricing and precise arrival timing — a challenge we master for every shipment.

Specifications
Bulk Supply Global Origin C3S ≥60% Free Lime ≤1.5%

GGBFS

Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag — primarily used by the ready-mix and precast concrete industries. GGBFS enhances concrete durability, reduces heat of hydration, and is highly valued in port, bridge, and marine construction projects.

Specifications
Grade 80 / Grade 100 EN 15167 Bagged & Bulk

GBFS (Granulated Slag)

A by-product of the steel industry with growing importance in cement production. GBFS reduces CO₂ emissions in the final product and is sourced from steel mills across the Far East, Europe, and beyond. Ideal for blended cement manufacturing.

Specifications
Granulated Form CaO >35% Low Carbon

Steel & Iron Products

Long steel, flat steel, and raw materials for construction and manufacturing industries

Steel Rebar (TMT)

High Demand

Thermo-mechanically treated reinforcement steel bars for reinforced concrete construction. Supplied in coils or straight lengths. Widely used in residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects across our partner markets.

Grades & Sizes
B500B (EN 10080) A615 Gr.60 8mm – 40mm 6m / 12m Bars

Steel Billets

Semi-finished steel billets for rolling mills producing rebar, wire rod, and other long products. Sourced from integrated steelmakers and EAF producers. Available in various section sizes and steel grades, including low-carbon and high-strength.

Specifications
130×130mm 150×150mm 160×160mm 3SP / 5SP Q235 / S235

Structural Steel

H-beams, I-beams, angle bars, channels, and flat bars for large-scale structural steel construction. Used in industrial buildings, warehouses, bridges, and infrastructure projects. Available in standard EN and ASTM profiles.

Profiles
H-Beam I-Beam / IPE Angles L Channels S235 / S355

Wire Rod

Coiled steel wire rod for drawing, mesh production, and industrial fastener manufacturing. Supplied from certified mills in Turkey, Russia, CIS, and China. Full mill certificates and chemical analysis provided with each lot.

Specifications
5.5mm – 16mm Low Carbon High Carbon SAE 1006 / 1008

Iron Ore & Pellets

Iron ore fines, lumps, and pellets for direct reduced iron (DRI) and blast furnace operations. We supply from established mining operations with consistent grade profiles and provide independent survey reports for every shipment.

Specifications
Fe ≥62% Fe ≥65% DRI Grade Pellets BF Grade

Steel Scrap

HMS 1&2 and shredded steel scrap for EAF and induction furnace steelmakers. We source from established recycling yards across Europe and North America, providing clean, sorted scrap with competitive pricing and reliable supply.

Grades
HMS 1&2 (80:20) HMS 1 (100%) Shredded Busheling
