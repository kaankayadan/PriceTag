Service 01

Procurement & Sourcing

Sourcing the right product at the right price requires deep market knowledge, established supplier relationships, and the ability to match technical specifications to commercial realities. Tyrian Global excels at exactly this.

Our procurement team works with factories across Europe, Turkey, Asia, and the CIS — giving us access to a wide range of product grades, production capacities, and pricing tiers to serve any project requirement.

  • Global supplier qualification and vetting
  • Technical specification matching and advisory
  • Competitive price discovery across multiple origins
  • Allocation of production capacity for large contracts
  • Regular market reports and price trend analysis
Service 02

Shipping & Logistics

International trade of bulk commodities demands expert logistics management. Tyrian Global handles the full freight cycle — from booking cargo space and coordinating loading to managing port agents and monitoring vessel progress.

Whether the cargo moves in bulk vessels, bagged containers, or tanker trucks, our logistics team ensures smooth operations and timely arrivals at your destination.

  • Full vessel chartering for bulk shipments
  • FCL/LCL container bookings with leading carriers
  • Port agency coordination worldwide
  • Real-time shipment tracking and status updates
  • Demurrage risk management and freight optimization
Service 03

Trade Documentation

International commodity trade requires a precise set of documents — and errors or omissions can delay cargo, block payment, or create legal complications. Our experienced documentation team ensures every document is correct, complete, and timely.

We are proficient in all major trade finance instruments and work seamlessly with banks, freight forwarders, and customs authorities worldwide.

  • Letters of Credit (LC) — issuance, negotiation, compliance
  • Bills of Lading, Airway Bills, CMR documents
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Weight Certificate
  • Certificates of Origin (EUR.1, Form A, CO)
  • Quality & quantity inspection certificates (SGS, Intertek, BV)
  • Customs export/import documentation
Service 04

Quality Assurance

Quality is not an afterthought at Tyrian Global — it is embedded at every stage of our supply process. We work exclusively with producers who hold the relevant international certifications, and we verify every shipment through independent, third-party inspection bodies.

Our quality process protects your investment and gives you confidence that what you ordered is exactly what you receive.

  • Pre-shipment inspection by SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas
  • Factory audit and production monitoring for large contracts
  • Laboratory testing of cement grades and steel compositions
  • Comparison of inspection results to purchase specification
  • Rejection and replacement management if quality deviates
